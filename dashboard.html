You don't have to be okay all the time.

Welcome to a little corner of the internet where you can pause, breathe, and let your feelings exist.

Life can feel overwhelming. Some days you may feel lost, exhausted, lonely, or unable to explain what's going on inside your mind. Sometimes you just need a place to let it out without worrying about being judged.

Write what you can't say out loud. Share the thoughts you've been keeping to yourself, or simply take a moment to breathe. You don't need to explain everything or pretend to be okay here.

You're allowed to feel what you feel. You deserve to be heard, and you don't have to face everything alone. Take your time. You're welcome here.

Words you might need today

Progress isn't always a straight line.

Dear you,

Sometimes we carry so many thoughts that we don't know where to put them. Here, you can let them out.

Write about what hurts. Write about what makes you tired. Write about the things you wish people understood about you. You can write to yourself, to someone you miss, to someone who hurt you, or to no one in particular. There's no right or wrong way, and it doesn't have to make sense.

Your letter stays with you. Nothing you write here is sent, saved, or read by anyone. It lives only on this page, so if you close or refresh it, it's gone. If you want to keep it, use "Copy my letter" and paste it somewhere private.

If you're in immediate danger or thinking about ending your life, please don't rely on this page. Contact local emergency services, a crisis line, or someone near you right now. See support options.

It's okay to reach out.

You don't have to figure everything out on your own. If your feelings become too heavy to carry, consider reaching out to someone you trust or a mental health professional. You deserve support, understanding, and care.

About depression

Depression is a real mental health condition, not a personal weakness or something you can simply "snap out of." It can look different for everyone.

Common experiences include a low or empty mood, losing interest in things you used to enjoy, changes in sleep or appetite, constant tiredness, trouble concentrating, and feeling worthless or hopeless.

If these have lasted for a couple of weeks or are getting in the way of daily life, a doctor or mental health professional can help. This page can't diagnose anyone.

Little things that might help

These aren't cures. They're small, optional supports for hard days.

  • Write it down, in a letter or a journal
  • Take a short walk or open a window
  • Listen to a song that feels like company
  • Let yourself rest without guilt
  • Message or sit near someone you trust

Find your people. Find your place.

You don't always need advice. Sometimes you just need someone to talk to, someone to laugh with, or someone who makes you feel included.

Join our community to connect with others, share your interests, and build friendships at your own pace. Whether you feel like talking, listening, playing games, or just being around people, there's room for you.

Community guidelines

  • No bullying, harassment, discrimination, or judgment.
  • Respect everyone's boundaries and privacy.
  • Never pressure anyone to share personal experiences.
  • Don't share another member's private messages or personal information without permission.
  • Members are peers, not necessarily trained mental health professionals.
  • Moderators respond to harmful content and to anyone who shares they are in crisis.
  • The server is not an emergency service. For urgent help, see Get Help.

This website offers emotional expression, mental health awareness, and community connection. It is not a substitute for professional mental health care or emergency services. You deserve support beyond this website, too.
